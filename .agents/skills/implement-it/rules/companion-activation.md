# Companion Activation

## Before implementation

Before writing or modifying code, enumerate the available implementation, testing, tooling, and
stack-companion skills that may apply to the requested issue. Do not stop after finding the first
matching skill, and do not assume one general framework or testing skill makes a custom companion
unnecessary.

For each candidate skill, inspect its activation or trigger description and activate every skill
whose stated scope matches the work. A matching stack companion remains applicable even when a Boost,
framework, or testing skill also applies; one does not substitute for another unless the applicable
skill explicitly says so. When a skill states that it must be loaded alongside another named skill,
treat that relationship as part of its activation condition and satisfy both sides.

Treat activation as a decision that must be made before implementation, not something to infer from
the implementation outcome afterward. When no custom stack companion applies, continue with the
applicable general implementation skills and do not invent a stack-specific substitute.

## Evidence of activation

Record the relevant activation decision in the working session: which candidate skills were
considered, which were activated, and why an apparently relevant candidate was not activated. This
record should be concise and need not repeat the full skill text.

If the available skill listing or trigger text is inaccessible, stop and report the limitation rather
than silently assuming the first visible implementation skill is sufficient.
